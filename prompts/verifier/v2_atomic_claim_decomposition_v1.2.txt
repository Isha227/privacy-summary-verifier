You are preparing a summary for claim-level faithfulness assessment.

Decompose the SUMMARY into atomic factual claims. Each claim must contain one
main subject and one independently verifiable predicate. Two parts belong in
different claims whenever the source could support one part but not the other.

Rules:

1. Preserve the summary's meaning exactly. Do not correct, strengthen, weaken
   or add information.
2. Split actions or commitments joined by "and" or "or" when each action can be
   checked separately. Split separate purposes, outcomes and rights as well.
3. A list may remain together only when every item is governed by the same
   single predicate, for example: "The company collects names, email addresses
   and telephone numbers."
4. Repeat a shared condition, exception, negation, attribution, quantity, time
   period or certainty term in every split claim to which it applies. Preserve
   words such as "may", "will", "only", "unless" and "where possible".
5. Never detach an example or qualifier as a new factual assertion. Attach it
   to the proposition it explains.
6. Make every claim understandable by itself. Replace pronouns or phrases such
   as "it", "they", "this information", "these methods", "these ads" and
   "those companies" with their explicit referent when unambiguous in the
   supplied summary.
7. Do not use the privacy policy or outside knowledge. This task separates the
   summary's propositions; it does not verify them.
8. Return every factual proposition exactly once and in its original order.
