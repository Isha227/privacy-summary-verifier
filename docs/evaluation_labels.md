# Evaluation labels

## Sentence source alignment

- Supported: the source supports the statement and important qualifications.
- Partially supported: the main idea has support, but scope or qualifications
  are changed or incomplete. This indicates distortion.
- Unsupported: the policy lacks sufficient support. This indicates hallucination.
- Contradicted: the source explicitly conflicts with the statement.

## Information coverage

- Covered: the summary retains the important source information.
- Partially covered: it retains the central idea but loses material detail.
- Not covered: the important information is absent.

## Taxonomy mapping

Mappings use the ten OPP-115 categories after source-unit extraction. Other is
a review outcome, not automatic proof that a new category is required.
