# Methodology in brief

Thirty privacy policies were processed with the same reproducible cleaning
procedure. Each was summarised by three model families, three prompting
strategies and two prompt versions, producing 540 summaries.

Accessibility was represented by readability and conciseness. Reliability was
assessed in two directions:

1. Summary to source: each generated sentence was checked for alignment.
2. Source to summary: important source information was checked for coverage.

Gemini performed evidence-citing alignment and coverage evaluation. Three
research-team members independently evaluated the contemporary subset before
disagreements were adjudicated. A source unit is one discrete item of policy
information that an ordinary reader may need, together with its source
evidence. Units were extracted before taxonomy mapping, preventing OPP-115
from determining what counted as important.

Inferential comparisons used the 27 OPP-115 policies. The three contemporary
policies were analysed separately for detailed human validation. For the
matched taxonomy comparison, the same refined Gemini extraction procedure
produced units for three OPP-115 and three contemporary policies; the human
units supplied a separate validation reference.
