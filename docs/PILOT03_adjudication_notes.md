# PILOT03 source-unit adjudication

The candidate alignment is a mechanical aid, not a final gold standard. Text
similarity can pair related but distinct propositions or miss equivalent units
worded differently. The three researchers must verify every row against their
original files and the frozen `PILOT03.txt`. Do not inspect generated summaries
during source-unit adjudication.

For each provisional row:

1. Check whether the listed A1/A2/A3 identifiers express the same proposition.
2. Move an identifier to another row or create a new row if the candidate match
   is wrong.
3. Set `agreement_status` to a descriptive value such as `unanimous`,
   `two annotators`, `one annotator`, `granularity disagreement`, or
   `category disagreement`.
4. Choose `retain`, `merge`, `split`, or `reject`.
5. For retained final units, assign an ID such as `P03-C1-U01`, enter neutral
   final wording, retain material qualifiers, and record a concise rationale.
6. For rejected units, leave final unit fields blank and explain why the item is
   duplicative, non-material, outside policy scope, or not independently
   scorable.

Pay particular attention to service-specific examples, controller distinctions,
legal bases, third-party recipients, retention exceptions, public-content
effects, international transfers, and regional rights. Avoid both excessive
fragmentation and units so broad that independent omissions cannot be scored.
