# Prototype guide

The prototype supports contemporary and OPP-115 sentence source alignment and
information coverage, automated–researcher agreement, and OPP taxonomy mapping.

Selecting a sentence or source unit reveals its evaluator label, reason and
linked policy evidence. Evaluator decisions remain separate rather than being
combined into an artificial overall label.

Other taxonomy mappings require review. A new category would be justified only
if important information describes a distinct privacy practice and recurs
across multiple policies. The contemporary analysis contains only three
policies, limiting conclusions about OPP-115 completeness for all current
policies.
