# PILOT02 adjudication meeting notes

The alignment contains 37 provisional concepts. It follows A1's finer list as
an organising spine and maps likely A2/A3 equivalents. It is not a final gold
standard; all decisions require agreement by the three researchers.

## Main issues to resolve

1. **Granularity and materiality:** decide whether browser-storage details,
   Smarter Encryption, public social commentary, recruiting-policy scope, and
   cross-border operational detail are independently important to a lay summary.
2. **Third-party detail:** preserve material commitments concerning Microsoft,
   hosting/content providers, SaaS vendors, and survey vendors without turning
   every example into a separate unit.
3. **Category differences:** resolve the external-site boundary (C5/C9) and the
   inability to disclose linked histories (C6/C3) without duplicating units.
4. **Qualifiers:** assess temporary use, anonymity/de-linking, zero-knowledge
   encryption, contractual access limits, legal-retention exceptions, legal
   contest commitments, and notice-prohibition exceptions.
5. **One-annotator units:** ADJ2-22, ADJ2-29, and ADJ2-33 need explicit source
   review and a materiality decision; they must not be accepted solely because
   one annotator used finer granularity.

## Procedure

For every row, choose `retain`, `merge`, `split`, or `reject`; assign final IDs
such as `P02-C1-U01`; write neutral final wording and material qualifiers; and
record a short rationale. Do not inspect generated summaries during this stage.
